# Lab 1: Training

### What the DeepLabCut GUI is used for

The DeepLabCut Graphical User Interface (GUI) provides an accessible way to create and manage pose estimation projects without writing code. Through the GUI you can:

- Create a new project and organize your videos  
- Extract frames and label body parts manually  
- Refine or correct labels visually  
- Train a neural network and monitor its progress  
- Evaluate model performance and inspect test results  
- Analyze new videos and export pose data  

In short, the GUI guides you through the entire workflow of building and applying a pose-estimation model, making the process easier and more intuitive for beginners.
