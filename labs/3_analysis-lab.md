# Lab 3: Single-animal analysis

## Introduction 🐭📈

In this exercise you will explore the pose-estimation output of a single mouse recorded in a controlled arena. Instead of working with video frames, you will analyze the predicted 2D coordinates generated by a pretrained SuperAnimal model. These predictions contain the tracked keypoints for every frame in the video along with likelihood values that indicate the model’s confidence.

👉🏼 You will work with previously cleaned data generated at the end of *Lab 2 EDA*

Your task is to gain an initial understanding of how these pose-tracking files are structured, what they contain and how the animal’s behavior is reflected in the movement of keypoints. You will compute simple movement metrics, visualize trajectories, inspect the quality of detections and begin to identify patterns that characterize the animal’s behavior. 

## 🎯 Goals

By the end of this exercise you will be able to:

• Load a single-animal pose-tracking dataset and inspect its structure.
• Understand the organization of coordinate arrays, likelihood scores and frame indexing.
• Visualize keypoint trajectories through time and in the arena space.
• Extract simple quantitative features such as speed, total displacement and path length.
• Interpret how pose-estimation output reflects behavioral dynamics.

## 📂 Deliverables

From this notebook you will generate:

- A set of visualizations showing keypoint positions over time.
- Basic behavioral metrics derived from pose coordinates, including speed and movement trajectories.
- Notes or observations about data quality issues you detect, such as missing points or abrupt jumps.
